About Us

The University of Calgary Debate Society is dedicated to fostering excellence in competitive debate and developing student leaders. We provide a supportive community where members can develop critical thinking, public speaking, and argumentation skills through participation in parliamentary, policy, and other debate formats.

Our club hosts regular meetings, workshops, and participates in regional and national tournaments. Whether you're a seasoned debater or completely new to the activity, we welcome all students interested in debate and intellectual discourse.
