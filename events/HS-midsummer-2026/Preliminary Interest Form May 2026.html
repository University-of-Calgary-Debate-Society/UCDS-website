Warm greetings from the University of Calgary Debate Society (UCDS)!

As we look ahead to the summer, we are currently in the planning stages of hosting a debate tournament on July 25. Before we finalize our arrangements, we are reaching out to gauge the level of interest from our community to ensure we create the best possible experience for our debaters.

Tournament Details

If we proceed, the tournament will feature the following structure:

Date & Format The event will be a one-day-only tournament hosted on Saturday, July 25, consisting of 4 rounds of debate (3 in-rounds and 1 out-round).
Categories There will be two open competitive categories: Junior High and Senior High. Debaters will be placed into the category corresponding to the grade they are completing in 2026.
Speaking Categories To ensure a supportive environment for all skill levels, we will offer separate speaking categories for Open and Beginner speakers for both Junior High and Senior High categories.
Teaming Hybrid teams will be allowed. Teams consisting of a Junior High student and Senior High student will compete in the Senior High Open category.
Judging & Chaperoning University debaters will serve as Chairs for the debate rounds, though parent judges may be needed depending on attendance. Please note that parents will be required to chaperone their children during the event.
IMPORTANT: This email is NOT a registration form. It is solely an expression of interest to help us plan effectively.
