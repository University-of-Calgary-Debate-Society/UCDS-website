Calgary Summer Cup 2026

The University of Calgary Debate Society welcomes you to the Calgary Summer Cup 2026! A 2-day online tournament held on Saturday, July 25th and Sunday, July 26th, is going to be on Discord. You can find more details in our tournament invite, and register through our registration form.

Schedule

Saturday, July 25

  • 8:30-9:00Check-in
  • 9:00-9:30Briefings (Debater, Judges, Equity, Tech)
  • 9:30-10:45Round 1 (in-round)
  • 10:45-12:00Round 2 (in-round)
  • 12:00-1:00Lunch Break
  • 1:00-1:15Check-in
  • 1:15-2:30Round 3 (in-round)
  • 2:45-4:00Round 4 (in-round)
  • 4:00-4:30Wrap-up

Sunday, July 26

  • 9:30-10:00Check-in and break announcements
  • 10:00-11:30Semi-Finals
  • 11:30-12:30Lunch & break announcements
  • 12:30-12:45Check-in
  • 12:45-2:30Finals
  • 2:30-3:30Awards and Announcements

Tournament Fee: $30 per debater. UCDS will be providing university judges. Parent judges will not be needed.
