Calgary Summer Cup

The University of Calgary Debate Society is pleased to welcome you to the Calgary Summer Cup 2026! This tournament will be held on July 25th online, over Discord.

This tournament is not associated with the ADSA.

Format & Platforms

Tournament Format

This tournament will consist of three in-rounds of British Parliamentary debate and one grand final. All in-rounds will be open adjudication, meaning you will be told your placement in the round immediately after the judge's deliberation. Speaker scores will not be revealed.

Online Platform

The tournament will be held on a Discord server, the link to which will be sent out before the tournament. Please create a Discord account if you do not already have one, or have a parent create one for you to use if you are under the age of 13. Tournament staff will be able to help you navigate the platform if you are not familiar with it.

Eligibility & Categories

Eligibility Scope: Open to all students enrolled in Grades 6-12 during the recently completed 2025-2026 academic year.

Junior High Division (Grades 6-8)

Students in grades 6, 7, and 8 who have not competed before are eligible for the Junior Beginner category, but may compete in the Junior Open category if they wish. Other junior high students must compete in Junior Open.

All junior high students will compete together in rounds, but awards and breaks will be separated.

Senior High Division (Grades 9-12)

Students in grades 9, 10, and 11 who have not competed before are eligible for the Senior Beginner category, but may compete in the Senior Open category if they wish. Other senior high students must compete in Senior Open.

All senior high students will compete together in rounds, but awards and breaks will be separated.

Hybrid & Mixed-Grade

Hybrid teams of debaters that completed different grades compete in the category of the most senior debater (e.g. grade 9 and 7 debates as Junior Open). Teams with a junior high and high school student compete in Senior.

Judging & Awards

Judging & Spectating

Judging will be provided by experienced university debaters, allowing for quality judging and personalized feedback. Parents and other spectators are permitted if all debaters in the room are comfortable with it.

Awards Categories

Awards will be given to teams and speakers in the following categories: Junior Beginner, Junior Open, Senior Beginner, Senior Open.
