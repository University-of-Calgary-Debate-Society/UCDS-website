Calgary Summer Cup

University of Calgary Debate Society

 

The University of Calgary Debate Society is excited to invite you to the Calgary Summer Cup 2026! This tournament will be held online over Discord from July 25th to July 26th.

Coaches, we would highly appreciate it if you could send this out to your debaters! The sign up form is also publicly accessible on our website ucds.ca.

⚠️ Please note: This tournament is not associated with the ADSA.
 

Tournament Details

Dates Saturday, July 25 & Sunday, July 26, 2026
Location Online (via Discord server)
Format British Parliamentary (BP) • Open Adjudication
Eligibility Students in Grades 6-12 during the 2025-2026 school year
Entry Fee $30 per debater (E-transfer to ucds.debate@gmail.com)
 

Division & Categories

Junior High (Grades 6-9)

Categories: Junior Beginner & Junior Open.
Beg. category is open to students who did not compete in Grade 6, 7 and 8.

 

Senior High (Grades 9-12)

Categories: Senior Beginner & Senior Open.
Beg. category is open to students who did not compete in Grade 10 or 11.

 

Key Deadlines

Registration Deadline: July 18th, 2026

Payment Deadline: July 20th, 2026
